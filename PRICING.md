# Vacha Pricing

Vacha is free for everyone: individuals, companies and organizations of any
size. It is open source under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or
(at your option) any later version ([LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt)), so you may
use, study, share and change it under that license's terms.

| What | Who | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vacha | Everyone | Free |
| Security updates until at least 31 December 2031 | Everyone | Free |
| Support agreement | Companies that want priority fixes or help with deployment | On request |
| Donation | Anyone who wants to support the work | Optional |

Notes:

- **Support agreements** are made in writing on request; contact
  info@nova-globen.se with what you need. They cover help and priority, not
  permission to use Vacha, which the license already gives everyone.
- **Donations** are voluntary and buy no rights or services. The donation
  link is not set up yet; it will be published at
  <https://didabit.com/vacha> when it is.
- Prices for support agreements exclude VAT. Business customers in the EU
  outside Sweden with a valid VAT number are invoiced under the
  reverse-charge rule; Swedish VAT is added for customers in Sweden.

Contact: info@nova-globen.se
