# Security

If you find a security vulnerability in Vacha, report it privately to
**info@nova-globen.se** with "Security" in the subject line, or through the
Nova Globen contact form. Please do not disclose it publicly before we have
released a fix.

Include the affected version (Help > About), the steps to reproduce, and
what an attacker could achieve. We acknowledge reports within five business
days and tell you what we will do and when.

Vulnerabilities in the underlying Code - OSS platform should also be
reported to Microsoft, following
<https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/blob/main/SECURITY.md>.
